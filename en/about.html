The Mahjoy Story

Mahjoy was born from a simple idea: if we loved gathering around the mahjong table, why couldn't the game be as beautiful as the moments it creates?

We're Monica and Marcela — two moms, designers, and lifelong lovers of thoughtful design based in Monterrey, Mexico. What started as a shared passion for mahjong quickly became a mission to reimagine the game with elevated aesthetics, exceptional quality, and details that make every game feel special.

When we couldn't find products that reflected our style, we decided to create them ourselves. The result was the first premium mahjong brand created in Mexico and Latin America — designed for players who appreciate craftsmanship, meaningful design, and the joy of gathering with friends and family.

Every Mahjoy collection is thoughtfully designed to bring personality and beauty to the table, blending timeless play with modern design. From our tiles to our accessories, every detail is created to make your mahjong experience as memorable as the people you play with.
